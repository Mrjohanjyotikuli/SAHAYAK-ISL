Bridging Silence with Real-Time Precision

SAHAYAK translates Indian Sign Language (ISL) spatial hand gestures into intelligible textual and spoken outputs in real-time. Built to empower over 63 million Deaf and Hard of Hearing individuals across India through high-accuracy landmark sequence classification.

How SAHAYAK Operates

Four-stage computational pipeline converting raw web camera frames to contextual text and voice output.

01

Frame Ingestion

Captures video stream at 30 FPS. Standardizes frames to consistent aspect ratios and applies CLAHE color normalization to adapt to fluctuating lighting conditions.

02

Landmark Extraction

Detects 21 3D spatial joint coordinates per hand. Extracts bounding box vectors and normalizes coordinates relative to wrist origin point.

03

Sequence Classification

Feeds temporal feature arrays into lightweight temporal neural networks (LSTM/1D-CNN) to evaluate frame sequence transitions.

04

Synthesis & Output

Filters transient noise using temporal thresholding, constructs natural language sentences, and dispatches audio synthesis.

Dataset Specifications

Indian Sign Language dataset composition and training metrics

Dataset Composition

  • Alphabet Classes (A-Z): 26 Signs
  • Numerical Digits (0-9): 10 Signs
  • Conversational Phrases: 50+ Key Expressions
  • Total Sample Sequences: 45,000+ Samples

Training Performance

  • Training Accuracy: 98.4%
  • Validation Accuracy: 96.2%
  • Average Inference Latency: ~42 ms
  • Lighting Resilience: CLAHE Enhanced
